My Story

See · Plan · Do · Review

When I take on a project, I see the messy, complicated parts clearly — and I know exactly how to turn them into something that works. I'm a Project Manager, Business Analyst and Operations Professional who bridges strategy, execution, and technology.

I plan, coordinate, and oversee projects end-to-end — ensuring timelines, budgets, and quality standards are met. I excel at managing cross-functional teams, aligning stakeholders, and keeping every detail on track across FMCG, tech, consulting, and nonprofit sectors.

I create structured workflows that reduce bottlenecks, improve communication, and increase operational efficiency. I leverage tools like Asana, Zapier, SAP, and Power BI to automate repetitive tasks, track performance, and make teams more productive.

Ask me anything

The questions people usually ask.

  1. 01What kind of problems do you like working on?

    The messy ones. When customer records, field activity and sales data are spread across separate tools, or reports arrive in five different formats, I like finding the structure underneath and building the system that makes it work.

  2. 02Project manager, business analyst or sales operations: which one are you?

    All three, and they feed each other. As a business analyst I turn business needs into clear requirements. As a project manager I plan and deliver the work end to end. In sales operations I see whether it actually holds up in the field, across reps, outlets and distributors.

  3. 03What does See · Plan · Do · Review look like on a real project?

    On Frutta SmartSeller: see how sales, visits and outlet coverage were really being captured; plan the requirements and rollout with the vendor; do the onboarding and training; review adoption and daily recording with sales leaders until it sticks.

  4. 04How do you get people to actually use a new system?

    Rollout is only half the job. I train people on their own workflows, stay close to their questions in the first weeks, resolve issues quickly and follow up on daily recording until it becomes habit. A system nobody updates is just an expensive spreadsheet.

  5. 05What do you do off the clock?

    Usually I’m still organising something. I co-founded Femme PWR, and I’ve worked with Girl Power Foundation and Junior Achievement Nigeria on programmes for women, girls and young people.
