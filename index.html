    Frutta SmartSeller — Implementation & Adoption

    Business requirements, commercial process alignment and vendor coordination for Frutta Foods’ SmartSeller implementation, followed by rollout, training and adoption support.

    View images ↗
    Project details
    Organisation
    Frutta Foods
    My role
    Head of Sales Operations · Product management responsibilities
    Focus
    CRM & SFA

    A purpose-built internal sales application for distributor, rep and outlet operations. The work connected commercial requirements with implementation, rollout and ongoing use.

    The business challenge

    Customer records, field activity and sales transactions were spread across separate tools. Frutta needed a shared platform that reflected its route structure, pricing rules and reporting needs.

    My contribution

    I owned the business requirements and commercial process alignment: how sales, customer visits, outlet coverage and performance should be captured and reviewed. I coordinated the vendor implementation, rollout, onboarding, training, issue resolution and adoption follow-up with sales leaders.

    Main activities and deliverables

    • Business requirements aligned with sales operations and reporting.
    • Vendor coordination, user onboarding and training.
    • Issue resolution, rollout support and follow-up on daily recording discipline.

    Sales operations & reporting

    Frutta Secondary Sales Dashboard

    The sales team operated without visibility into SKU-level performance. Decisions were made on gut feel, not data — leading to stockouts on high-demand products and overstocking on slow movers.

    View images ↗
    Project details
    Organisation
    Frutta Foods
    My role
    Sales operations and reporting
    Focus
    Reporting & analytics

    A central sales-reporting view for understanding SKU performance, field activity and territory results in an FMCG operation.

    The business challenge

    Manual reports arrived in inconsistent formats. Management needed a clearer view of products, reps and regions rather than relying on disconnected summaries or gut feel.

    My contribution

    I mapped the sales data pipeline from field reports to management decisions. I focused on data structure and accessibility, designed a normalised data model and defined business KPIs including MTD revenue, cases sold, outlet coverage and rep compliance.

    Main activities and deliverables

    • Centralised SKU-level reporting.
    • Rep-performance tracking and territory comparisons.
    • Regional achievement breakdowns and ASM-level filtering.
    • Performance views that identify top and bottom performers.

    Reporting outcome

    The dashboard brings product, field and territory reporting into a shared management view. Figures visible in the approved capture describe its reporting period; they are not revenue attributed to my work.

    Tools and platforms

    • Advanced Excel
    • Power BI
    • Google Sheets
    • Data modelling
    • GitHub Pages

    Girl Power Foundation — Website & Digital Presence

    The Girl Power Foundation Nigeria had no digital presence to amplify their mission of empowering women and girls — limiting their reach, donor engagement, and volunteer recruitment.

    View images ↗
    Project details
    Organisation
    Girl Power Foundation Nigeria
    My role
    Project Manager
    Focus
    Non-profit & digital

    A website and digital-presence project for a foundation focused on women’s empowerment and girl-child development.

    The objective

    The foundation needed a professional place to explain its mission, present programmes and make it easier for donors, volunteers and potential partners to engage. Informal communication alone did not give those audiences a reliable reference point.

    My contribution

    I led the project from requirements gathering with the founding team through information architecture, content strategy, vendor coordination and launch. The focus was on communicating the mission clearly and making participation easy to understand.

    Main activities and deliverables

    • Programme showcases and a clear presentation of the foundation’s mission.
    • Donation and volunteer sign-up paths.
    • Team profiles and an events section.
    • Project planning, content coordination and QA before launch.

    Project outcome

    The delivered website gave the foundation a digital identity and a place to present its programmes, donation paths and volunteer participation routes.
