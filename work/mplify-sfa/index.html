Mplify SFA — Field Sales Automation

A field-sales automation project for structuring rep visits, customer activity and performance reporting.

My role
SFA implementation and platform management
Focus
SFA · Field operations

The business challenge

Manual check-ins and incomplete or late end-of-day reports made it difficult to verify visits, monitor compliance or connect field activity with sales performance. The project needed a more structured way to record and review day-to-day field work.

My contribution

I implemented and managed the Mplify SFA platform. I defined KPIs for visit compliance, clock-in tracking and activity monitoring, and built dashboards connecting field data to sales outcomes across activities, visits, rep performance and target tracking.

Main activities and deliverables

  • Daily customer-activity and visit-completion tracking.
  • Clock-in verification and rep-performance scoring.
  • A reps locator and customer tracking.
  • Dashboards for activities, visits, performance and targets.

Operational outcome

The platform provided a structured view of field activity, visit completion and sales performance for operational review.

Tools and platforms

  • Mplify SFA
  • Activity dashboards
  • CRM integration
  • Field analytics
