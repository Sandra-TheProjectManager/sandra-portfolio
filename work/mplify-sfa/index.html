SFA · Field operationsMplify SFA: Field Sales Automation

Proving the visit happened

Field work was recorded through manual check-ins and end-of-day reports that were often incomplete or late. That made it hard to verify visits, measure compliance or link activity to sales. I implemented and managed the Mplify SFA platform to change that.

My role

SFA implementation, KPIs and dashboards

I owned
  • Implementing and managing the Mplify SFA platform
  • KPIs for visit compliance, clock-ins and activity
  • Dashboards linking field data to sales
Focus
SFA · Field operations

From reported to recorded

Before

  • Manual check-ins
  • End-of-day reports, often late or incomplete
  • Visits hard to verify

With the platform

  • Clock-in verification
  • Daily activity and visit-completion tracking
  • A reps locator and customer tracking

Choosing what to measure

Before building any views, I defined the KPIs: visit compliance, clock-in tracking and activity monitoring. Those decided what the platform needed to capture each day.

Connecting activity to sales

I built dashboards that tied field data to sales outcomes, across activities, visits, rep performance scoring and target tracking. Field activity became something managers could review against results, not just a log.

What it gave the team

A structured, daily view of field activity, visit completion and sales performance for operational review.

Tools and platforms

  • Mplify SFA
  • Activity dashboards
  • CRM integration
  • Field analytics
