Girl Power Foundation — Website & Digital Presence

A website and digital-presence project for a foundation focused on women’s empowerment and girl-child development.

Organisation
Girl Power Foundation Nigeria
My role
Project Manager
Focus
Non-profit & digital

The objective

The foundation needed a professional place to explain its mission, present programmes and make it easier for donors, volunteers and potential partners to engage. Informal communication alone did not give those audiences a reliable reference point.

My contribution

I led the project from requirements gathering with the founding team through information architecture, content strategy, vendor coordination and launch. The focus was on communicating the mission clearly and making participation easy to understand.

Main activities and deliverables

  • Programme showcases and a clear presentation of the foundation’s mission.
  • Donation and volunteer sign-up paths.
  • Team profiles and an events section.
  • Project planning, content coordination and QA before launch.

Project outcome

The delivered website gave the foundation a digital identity and a place to present its programmes, donation paths and volunteer participation routes.

Tools and methods

  • WordPress
  • Project planning
  • Content strategy
  • Stakeholder management
  • QA testing

Beyond the website

  • Led 10 programmes empowering young girls through mentorship, education and career development.
  • Grew programme participation by 50%, reaching more underserved communities.
  • Secured partnerships with NGOs and corporate brands, adding programme resources.
  • Organised an advocacy event that drew 150+ attendees.
  • Streamlined project processes, cutting execution timelines by 40%.
