Technology · Project deliveryHelpApp Africa

Four teams, one launch

HelpApp Africa connects people who need everyday services with verified providers. Getting the platform live meant technical, marketing, operations and support teams moving in step. As Project Manager, I kept them aligned through to go-live and beyond.

My role

Cross-functional delivery and go-live

I owned
  • Go-live planning, testing and deployment readiness
  • Workflows, SOPs and reporting structures
  • Pre- and post-launch marketing direction
I coordinated
  • Technical, marketing, operations and support teams
  • The technical team through the development lifecycle

Why coordination was the job

Finding, verifying and booking service providers had relied on word of mouth and informal contacts. HelpApp set out to make that structured. Building it involved four teams, each with its own priorities and timelines.

My work connected them around one delivery plan. It is distinct from personally developing the software.

The road to go-live

  1. Build

    Supervised the technical team, keeping business requirements and timelines aligned.

  2. Test

    Coordinated testing and followed issues through to resolution.

  3. Ready

    Deployment readiness and pre-launch marketing.

  4. Live

    Launch, then post-launch operations and support.

What was delivered

A live platform for discovering, verifying and booking service providers, with launch marketing and the workflows needed to run it after go-live.
