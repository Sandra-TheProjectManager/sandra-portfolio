HelpApp Africa

A technology-platform project connecting people who need everyday services with professional service providers.

Organisation
HelpApp Africa
My role
Project Manager · cross-functional coordination and testing
Focus
Technology · Project delivery
Visit website (opens in a new tab) ↗

The objective

The project addressed a fragmented service-provider market, where people relied on word-of-mouth and informal contacts. The platform aimed to make finding, verifying and booking service providers more structured and easier to use.

My contribution

I coordinated the technical, marketing, operations and support workstreams and testing. My project-management work connected those teams around delivery workflows and platform readiness; it is distinct from personally developing the software.

Main activities and deliverables

  • Coordination across technical, marketing, operations and support teams.
  • Testing coordination and follow-through across workstreams.
  • Delivery workflows connecting platform work with operational readiness.

Platform context

The public HelpApp Africa website presents service discovery, service-provider verification and booking. The approved homepage capture and live site show the platform’s public-facing experience.
