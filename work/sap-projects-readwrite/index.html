SAP Projects — ReadWrite Data Solutions

SAP work at ReadWrite Data Solutions, including a Business One ERP engagement focused on connecting inventory, procurement, finance and reporting workflows.

Organisation
ReadWrite Data Solutions
My role
Operations and project delivery · SAP Business One implementation and management
Focus
SAP · Business operations

The business challenge

Inventory, procurement, financials and reporting were managed across disconnected tools. Finance, warehouse and operations teams worked from different data sources, with duplicated spreadsheet work and manual reconciliation.

My contribution

I led the SAP Business One implementation and ongoing management via Cloudiax, covering data migration, user onboarding, process design and reporting configuration. I worked to align the environment with actual business workflows, using HANA Studio, Crystal Reports and DTW for data imports.

Main activities and deliverables

  • Inventory-management and procurement workflows.
  • Financial reporting and user-access management.
  • Data imports, migration and user onboarding.
  • Ongoing environment management across Floating Panel, Crystal Report, DTW and Company Share modules.

Operational outcome

The work brought operations, finance and warehouse information into an integrated ERP environment.

Tools and platforms

  • SAP Business One
  • HANA Studio
  • Crystal Reports
  • Cloudiax
  • DTW

Operations leadership at ReadWrite

  • 30+ SAP S/4HANA and BW projects delivered, from data infrastructure rollouts to corporate data-literacy training.
  • Workflows managed for a portfolio of 50+ corporate client accounts.
  • 100% on-time delivery for two consecutive quarters, leading developers, analysts, trainers and consultants.
  • Automation that removed 50% of manual processes and saved 200+ hours a month.
  • Client retention up 35%, contributing to 20% revenue growth within six months.
