Getting finance, warehouse and operations onto one system

Inventory, procurement, finance and reporting were handled in disconnected tools, so each team worked from its own numbers. I led an SAP Business One implementation to bring them together, alongside running operations at ReadWrite Data Solutions.

Fitting the system to the work

I led the Business One implementation and its ongoing management via Cloudiax: data migration, user onboarding, process design and reporting configuration.

The guiding rule was to map the environment to how the business actually ran, rather than make the business bend to the software. DTW handled data imports, and HANA Studio and Crystal Reports supported the data and reporting work.

Running the operation around it

As Head of Operations, I oversaw workflows for more than 50 corporate client accounts and led delivery of 30+ SAP S/4HANA and BW projects, from data infrastructure rollouts to corporate data-literacy training.

Workflow automation removed 50% of manual processes and saved over 200 hours a month without adding headcount. Client retention rose by 35%, contributing to 20% revenue growth within six months.
