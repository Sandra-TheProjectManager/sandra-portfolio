Frutta Secondary Sales Dashboard

A central sales-reporting view for understanding SKU performance, field activity and territory results in an FMCG operation.

Organisation
Frutta Foods
My role
Sales operations and reporting
Focus
Reporting & analytics

The business challenge

Manual reports arrived in inconsistent formats. Management needed a clearer view of products, reps and regions rather than relying on disconnected summaries or gut feel.

My contribution

I mapped the sales data pipeline from field reports to management decisions. I focused on data structure and accessibility, designed a normalised data model and defined business KPIs including MTD revenue, cases sold, outlet coverage and rep compliance.

Main activities and deliverables

  • Centralised SKU-level reporting.
  • Rep-performance tracking and territory comparisons.
  • Regional achievement breakdowns and ASM-level filtering.
  • Performance views that identify top and bottom performers.

Reporting outcome

The dashboard brings product, field and territory reporting into a shared management view. Figures visible in the approved capture describe its reporting period; they are not revenue attributed to my work.

Tools and platforms

  • Advanced Excel
  • Power BI
  • Google Sheets
  • Data modelling
