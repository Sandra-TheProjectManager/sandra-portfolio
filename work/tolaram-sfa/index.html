SFA · FMCG operationsTolaram SFA

When end-of-week reports arrive too late

Across brands and regions, field reports were disconnected and summarised weekly. Route adherence, outlet visits and rep productivity needed a clearer, more current view. My work in the Multipro/Tolaram field operation focused on what to track, getting teams ready, and turning field data into review.

My role

CRM/SFA rollout, tracking structures and reporting

I owned
  • Tracking structures for visits, routes, coverage and productivity
  • Training and change management for field teams
  • Operational and executive dashboards
I coordinated
  • Deployment and adoption across sales territories
  • Projects with Sales, Distribution and Technical teams
Organisation
Multipro / Tolaram field operations
Focus
SFA · FMCG operations

Why the weekly view fell short

Reports were split across brands and regions and arrived as end-of-week summaries. By the time a manager saw a missed route or an unvisited outlet, the week had moved on.

Deciding what to track

Customer visits
Was the outlet actually visited?
Route compliance
Did the rep follow the planned route?
Outlet coverage
Are we reaching the outlets we planned to?
Field productivity
What did each day in the field produce?

Bringing the field team with it

A new system changes how reps spend their day, so the rollout was as much about people as software. I coordinated deployment across sales territories and ran onboarding, training, issue resolution and change management.

The aim was steady CRM compliance and reporting discipline, not a one-off launch.

From field data to review

I built operational and executive dashboards from CRM, SFA, MFA and ERP data. They covered KPIs, territory performance, stock movement and sales team productivity, so field activity fed directly into operational review.

Platform and working methods

  • Sales force automation
  • Route planning and outlet mapping
  • Field analytics and performance reporting
